# Categories

The vocabulary lives in `src/jyyfinhub_spendtracker/categories.py`. This file is only for picking
when more than one fits.

## Boundaries

- Housing: the apartment itself. Rent, electricity, renters insurance.
- Utility: a provider I chose. Internet, mobile.
- Tech: equipment and infrastructure I run.
- Education: learning. `Tuition` is institutional, `Self Study` is bought directly.
- Leisure: fun. Lifestyle: upkeep.

## Tiebreaks

- Education or Tech: does it teach me, or help me produce?
- Housing or Utility: comes with the walls, or a provider I picked?
- Leisure or Lifestyle: would I miss it, or just replace it?

## Flat categories

- Only breakdown categories into sub-categories when analysis is useful.
- Example: how much of restaurant payments are from fast food vs. dining out?
- `Groceries` and `Transit` have no subcategories on purpose (I don't see the need to breakdown and analyze further as everything under I basically consider "essential")
